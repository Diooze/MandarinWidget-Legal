Help and contact

Find answers to common questions about MandarinWidget.

Why only 20 words?

The Free version keeps 20 HSK 1 words selected at first launch, shared between French and English. Open the Plus store page for the 5,400-word catalog; purchase availability depends on your platform.

How do I restore Plus?

On a supported platform, open the Plus store page and select Restore purchases. Use the same store account as the original purchase. Store purchases do not automatically transfer between Apple and Google.

How do I reset my progress?

Open Settings and choose “Reset progress”. Confirm your choice. Preferences and the selection of 20 free words are preserved.

My widget is not updating

Make sure the widget is installed correctly, the app is up to date and the widget settings are appropriate. Refresh delays may differ between iOS and Android. On iPhone, reopen MandarinWidget after its scheduled words end to start a new series.

Questions and bug reports

To contact us, use the support email below. For bug reports, include the app version, device, steps to reproduce the issue and, if possible, a screenshot without sensitive information.
