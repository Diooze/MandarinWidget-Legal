Terms of use

The information below describes how MandarinWidget is intended to operate and the rules for using it.

Draft — These are working documents not yet approved for official release. The publisher's legal identity and address, third-party SDKs, and applicable obligations still need verification.

1. Service

MandarinWidget provides Chinese vocabulary, review exercises and widgets to support learning. It is an educational aid and does not issue certifications.

2. Free access

The free plan provides 20 randomly selected HSK 1 words, kept on the device, and access to reviews and widgets for those words.

3. Plus and purchases

Where available on your platform, MandarinWidget Plus unlocks the full catalog of 5,400 HSK 1–6 words as a non-consumable, one-time purchase without a subscription. The intended price in France is €5.99; the amount actually charged is the price shown by your app store before purchase.

Payments and restoration are handled by Apple or Google depending on the platform and product availability. Access requires a verified active entitlement; a refund or revocation may remove Plus access without deleting local learning progress. Apple purchases do not automatically unlock Google Play purchases. Applicable consumer rights, including statutory guarantees and any withdrawal rights, remain unaffected.

4. Use and availability

Use of the app must comply with applicable law. Features such as widgets and text-to-speech depend on device capabilities and settings.

Fixes and changes to the service may be necessary. Content is not a guarantee of learning outcomes.

5. Content and intellectual property

MandarinWidget’s presentation and code belong to their respective rights holders. Some language data and resources come from third-party projects, including CC-CEDICT, CFDICT and Complete HSK Vocabulary. Attribution, licenses, source links and adaptations appear under About → Sources and licenses.

6. Publisher and final terms

The publisher’s legal identity, required contact information, applicable sales disclosures and detailed withdrawal-right information must be finalized before the first commercial release.
