MandarinWidget · Legal information

Privacy policy

This notice explains how MandarinWidget uses information required for its learning features and widgets.

Draft — These are working documents not yet approved for official release. The publisher's legal identity and address, third-party SDKs, and applicable obligations still need verification.

1. Publisher and controller

MandarinWidget is a Mandarin learning app. The publisher’s legal identity and contact details, as controller for any personal data handled by the service, must be completed before release. The official contact appears below.

2. Information used

The app stores interface preferences, the 20 selected free words, learning progress, exercise results, review schedules and widget settings locally on your device.

No MandarinWidget account is required. Your learning progress is not synchronized to a MandarinWidget account server.

3. Purposes

This information is used to display available words, remember progress, schedule reviews, customize the interface and update widgets.

The reviewed application code includes no ad network, marketing analytics or remote user account. This must be confirmed against the final iOS and Android builds, including third-party components.

4. Storage and technical services

Learning information is mainly stored on the device, including app storage and shared areas required for widgets. iOS or Android backup behavior may depend on your system settings.

Text-to-speech and widgets use operating-system services. In-app purchases, restoration and verification of Plus ownership use the relevant store (Apple App Store or Google Play where offered). These stores, not MandarinWidget, handle payments and bank details under their own privacy policies.

5. Retention and deletion

The “Reset progress” control clears learning results, review schedules and exercise statistics. It does not automatically erase preferences, selected free words or system-managed backups.

Uninstalling the app may erase local learning data. System-managed backups may remain depending on your device settings. Eligible purchases remain associated with the relevant store account and may be restored on the same platform.

6. Your rights

Where the GDPR applies, you may have rights to access, rectify or erase personal data, as well as other rights under applicable law.

Contact the address below to ask about your data or exercise your rights. If you contact support, your email address and message are used to reply; the exact retention period and applicable legal bases must be confirmed with the publisher before release. You may also contact the French data protection authority, the CNIL.

7. Changes

This policy will be updated when the data handled, third-party services or features change. A public version accessible without login must be available through the store listing before release.
